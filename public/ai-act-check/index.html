Is this AI use case allowed?

A Claude skill that screens an AI idea against the EU AI Act (as amended by the 2026 Digital Omnibus) and GDPR: risk tier, obligations with dates, safeguards and open questions.

Runs on Google Gemini 2.5 Flash with the skill as instructions

The report appears here. The three examples show stored reports; describe your own case and press Check it for a live screening.

The skill

One SKILL.md plus two reference files (AI Act tiers and timeline, GDPR checklist). Claude loads it whenever someone asks whether an AI idea is allowed.
